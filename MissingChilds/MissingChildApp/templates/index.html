info About Our System

Deep Learning Recognition

Our system uses advanced deep learning methodology for identifying reported missing children from available photos, using face recognition with over 95% accuracy.

Public Participation

The public can upload photographs of children they believe may be missing, along with location details. These photos are automatically compared with our database using AI algorithms.

Advanced Technology

Face descriptors are extracted using pre-trained CNN models with VGG-Face deep architecture, while child recognition is performed using a trained SVM classifier for maximum accuracy.
